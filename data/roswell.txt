The Roswell Incident (July 1947)

In mid-1947, a United States Army Air Forces balloon crashed at a ranch near Roswell, New Mexico. Following wide initial interest in the crashed "flying disc", the US military stated that it was merely a conventional weather balloon.

Major Jesse Marcel, an intelligence officer, was involved in the initial recovery of the debris. Decades later, ufologists began promoting a variety of increasingly elaborate conspiracy theories, claiming that one or more alien spacecraft had crash-landed and that the extraterrestrial occupants had been recovered by the military, who then engaged in a massive cover-up.  

In 1994, the US Air Force published a report identifying the crashed object as a nuclear test surveillance balloon from Project Mogul. Conspiracy theorists maintain that Project Mogul was a cover story designed to hide the recovery of non-human technology and biological entities.
